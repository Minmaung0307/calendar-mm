# Sources and verification — 2026-10-10

## Calendar engine and today's date
- Yan Naing Aye, MIT-licensed mmcal (version 20260909): https://github.com/yan9a/mmcal
- Reference calendar: https://yan9a.github.io/mmcal/m.htm
- 10 October 2026 newspaper, title “61st Myanma Gems Emporium commences”; masthead indexed as “14th Waning of Tawthalin 1388 ME”: https://www.moi.gov.mm/nlm/file-download/download/public/5506
- Result: 1388 ME, Tawthalin waning 14, sabbath eve. Myanmar civil lunar dates do not necessarily equal astronomical moon-phase instants.

## Public holidays
- DICA / Myanmar Companies Online, 2026 Business Calendar: https://www.myco.dica.gov.mm/public/Holidays.aspx?lang=en-US
- ASEAN–Japan Centre 2026 calendar, including April 11–19: https://www.asean.or.jp/en/learn-about-asean-japan/public-holidays-myanmar/
- Official Myanmar eVisa, Eid Al Adha announcement (28 May 2026): https://evisa.moip.gov.mm/?tick=605ff2e4
- Cross-check: https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/myanmar/2026
- The MOFA page currently shows 30 May for Kason; this conflicts with MyCO, ASEAN–Japan and the Myanmar-calendar conversion. This project uses 30 April 2026 (Kason full moon). 30 May is Nayon full moon.
- Diwali 8 November 2026 is an observance awaiting confirmation here, NOT treated as an established public holiday. Additional announcements require a data update.
- Two occasions coincide on 2 March; the UI keeps both names on one date.
- Other years: recurring Gregorian and Myanmar lunar rules, explicitly labelled as calculated, not a complete gazetted list. Eid, Deepavali, Chinese New Year and substitute holidays are not guessed for those years.
- An annual list of holidays does not imply a business-day/payroll calendar; weekend working-day substitutions are outside its scope.

## Monthly festivals and birthstones
- Myanmar National Portal seasonal festivals: https://www.myanmar.gov.mm/en/web/guest/festivals
- Ministry of Hotels and Tourism: https://tourism.gov.mm/en/festivals
- Ananda festival: https://tourism.gov.mm/en/festivals/Ananda%20Pagoda%20Festival
- Shwedagon festival descriptions: https://www.shwedagonpagoda.org.mm/en/festivals/
- Dates for Mahathamaya, Metta, Garudhamma and Authors' Day follow the same mmcal observance rules. Seasonal festivals without a single nationwide date remain month-level descriptions, not invented day assignments.
- GIA birthstones: https://www.gia.edu/birthstones
- Original poems are restored verbatim from the user's supplied public/data/poems.json; no new author attribution is invented.
